Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about digital products.

What is a digital planner?

A digital planner is a downloadable file you can use on a compatible device or print at home. Each product listing will explain its format and what is included.

Will anything be shipped to me?

No physical item is included. DAYFORM products are designed as digital downloads.

Can I print the pages?

Yes, the planner preview is designed for printing on A4 paper. Check each product's listing for its exact page size and format.

Can I buy products on this website right now?

This is the first version of the DAYFORM website and does not have checkout enabled yet. Product information is shown as a preview while the shop is being prepared.
